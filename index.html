Privacy Policy

Odomo · Last updated: 11 October 2026

Odomo is a vehicle expense and maintenance app for Android. This policy explains what the app stores, what is sent to Google Firebase, and how you can delete your data. Odomo does not require an account. We do not sell your data.

Information stored on your device

Odomo saves the details you enter, on the phone or tablet where the app is installed:

  • Your name and email address, used only to show your profile inside the app.
  • Vehicles: name, make, model, license plate, year, and odometer reading.
  • Expenses: category, title, description, amount, date, odometer, fuel amount, price per unit, and an optional place note.
  • Maintenance reminders, including dates and mileage.
  • Preferences: currency, distance unit, fuel unit, theme, and which notifications are enabled.

The place note is text you type. Odomo does not request GPS permission and does not read your device location.

Analytics and crash reports

Odomo uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics so we can understand whether the app is working and fix crashes. These services may receive technical information such as:

  • Device model, operating system version, and app version.
  • A rough region derived from your IP address.
  • App usage events, such as that the app was opened.
  • Crash diagnostics, including a build type label (for example, release).

Your vehicle records, expenses, name, and email are not uploaded to a server that we operate. Google processes the technical data above under the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

Advertising

This version of Odomo does not show ads. A future version may show ads, for example through Google AdMob. If ads are added, the advertising provider may collect or receive an advertising ID and limited technical data, such as device type, app version, and a rough region, in order to display ads and measure whether they were shown. Your vehicle records, expenses, name, and email will not be shared with advertisers. This page will be updated before ads are released, including the name of the advertising provider and a link to its privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Odomo schedules reminders on your device for maintenance, spending alerts, and a monthly report. Scheduling happens on the device. You can turn each reminder type off in Settings, or revoke the notification permission in Android settings.

Export and sharing

Export Data creates a CSV file on your device and opens the Android share sheet. The file is sent only to the app or destination you choose, such as email or cloud storage.

Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Google may include Odomo data in your device backup, or when you move to a new phone. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings. Odomo does not offer its own cloud account.

Deleting your data

In Settings, Reset All Data deletes your vehicles, expenses, reminders, profile, and preferences from the app. Uninstalling Odomo also removes its local data, except for a copy that may remain in an Android backup you already created. You can manage or delete device backups from your Google account.

Children

Odomo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
